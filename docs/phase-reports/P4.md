## 3. Record scope (service layer, every read and write)
- **Inquiries.** Customer: own. Agent: handled by her, or from a customer in her customer scope. Team Leader: from a customer in the team's scope (this includes unrouted customers, decision D11) or handled by a team agent. Manager: all. Only the handler, or a Team Leader / the Manager in scope, can answer, convert or close; another agent can read an inquiry from her customer but not act on it (403, audited).
- **Follow-ups.** Agent: tasks assigned to her. Team Leader: hers and her team's. Manager: all. Creating needs the customer in the creator's customer scope. An agent can assign only to herself; a Team Leader to herself or her team's agents; the Manager to any active staff member. Only the assignee (or a supervisor in scope) changes a task.
- **Staff cases.** The customer must be in the creator's customer scope; a Team Leader can pick only her team's agents.
- Outside the scope a page is 404 and a forged POST is 404 and changes nothing. Forbidden actions are 403 and audited as `access_denied`; refused status changes by customers/staff stay audited as before.

## 7. Where the build may differ from your diagrams (please check)
1. **Inquiry statuses** are Open, Answered, Converted to case, Closed (the sample said New, Answered, Converted). Closing is possible only for an answered inquiry.
2. **A customer asks one question and gets one answer.** There is no reply thread; if the answer is not enough the customer submits a complaint/request, or staff convert the inquiry to a case.
3. **"Convert to case" creates a new case and keeps the inquiry** (status Converted, link both ways); it does not turn the inquiry row into a case. The case type is chosen by staff (default Inquiry).
4. **Assigning an inquiry can also assign the customer** (only when the customer has no agent), so the handler can open the customer.
5. **Staff-recorded inquiries make the recorder the handler** and add a timeline interaction. Staff-created cases do the same (call / visit / SMS / chat / email).
6. **An Agent who creates a case is its agent**; a Team Leader or Manager may leave it unrouted in the Customer Queue.
7. **Follow-up "Type" and inquiry "Topic" are not built** (no columns; see §2 for the proposed SQL). Outcome and cancellation reasons are kept as dated lines in the task's notes.
8. **"Overdue" is derived, not a stored status**; the stored statuses are Pending, Done, Cancelled.
9. **Follow-ups are linked to a case** (optional). Linking to an order waits for P5 (`sales_order_id` exists).
10. **Interaction timeline:** inquiries and cases recorded by staff appear in it; portal-submitted inquiries do not (they are their own list).
