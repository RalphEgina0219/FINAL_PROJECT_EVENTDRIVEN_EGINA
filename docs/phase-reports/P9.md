## 1. What was done
| Area | What now works |
|---|---|
| **The rules for every figure** | **Real data only.** **Official figures exclude demo data** (`is_demo = 1`), **cancelled orders** and **voided sales**, and net sales subtract recorded refunds on the day they were recorded. Each analytics page has an **"Include demo data" switch**; when it is on, the page says **"includes demo data"** (not official). Without it, the page says how many demo rows were left out, with a link to include them. Days are **Asia/Manila** days and money is **Philippine pesos**. The period is chosen on the page (default: the last 30 days). |
| **Analytics › Overview** (`/crm/analytics`) | Net sales, average sale, orders placed (and how many were cancelled), new customers, cases opened and resolved, first reply on time, customer rating, refunds, a net-sales-per-day chart, and the next-7-days **Estimate**. |
| **Sales intelligence** | Net sales per day, sales by channel, top products (units and revenue), revenue by category, promotion codes used, loyalty points earned and redeemed, verified payments by method, and a top-products table. |
| **Customers & retention** | Customers who bought, **repeat customer rate**, loyalty members and points, customers with cases, buyers by number of purchases, new customers per month, top customers by spend, and **customer segments** (below). |
| **Forecasts (Estimate)** | **Statistical methods only, no AI or machine learning:** a 7-day moving average, a straight-line trend (least squares over 28 days) and "same weekday last week". For each of three metrics (net sales per day, orders per day, new cases per day), the method that would have been closest over the last 7 known days (lowest **MAE**) is used. Every run **stores** the method, method version, input period (56 complete days), horizon (14 days), error metric and value, what was excluded, and who ran it. Each day stores its estimate and a range (± 1.96 × back-test RMSE, never below zero). The chart shows **28 days of actual figures and then the Estimate columns**, dashed and labelled. The Manager presses **Compute new forecasts**, with an optional "include demo data" run that is labelled as such. Every run is audited. |
| **Customer segments** | `customer_segments` already held five rule-based segments, created in migration 104 from the master prompt. **No migration was needed.** Their written rules are now applied to each customer's own completed sales and interactions: loyal (6 or more sales in 6 months), regular (purchases in 2 of the last 3 months), at risk (a repeat customer with no purchase for 60–180 days), new (first purchase or registration in the last 30 days), inactive (nothing in 180 days). They are checked in that order; a customer who matches none has no segment. They are recomputed by the 5-minute job, after the demo seed, and by **Recompute segments now**. The customer page shows the segment. I first wrote a new migration with segments of my own, saw that the existing ones already defined the rules, and removed mine before it was ever applied anywhere. |
| **Dashboards for every role, real data only** | The Manager's last sample widget ("Retention and forecast": 62%, invented numbers) is replaced by the **real 90-day repeat-customer rate** and the **next-7-days Estimate** from the latest forecast run, labelled when demo data is included. Team Leader, Agent and Customer dashboards were already real (P5, P8). The dashboard note now says that dashboards are operational and include demo orders (labelled), and that the official figures are in Analytics. Reports (Manager and Team Leader) were already computed from real cases. |

## 5. Differences from your diagrams
1. **Forecast flow:** the Manager presses "Compute new forecasts"; there is no automatic nightly run. Each run picks one of three statistical methods by back-test. If a diagram shows a single fixed method or an automatic schedule, it differs.
2. **Segments** are computed by rules from the master prompt's five segments (no AI), not chosen by staff.
3. **Official vs. demo figures:** analytics exclude demo data by default and can include it with a visible label. Dashboards are operational and include demo orders with a label.

## 6. Open points
- With only demo data, official figures are zero (correct, and the page says so). Run `seed:reset` and enter some real orders, or use "Include demo data" for a demonstration.
- Forecasts need history: with fewer than 56 days of real data, the early days count as zero, which pulls the estimate down. The input period is printed with every run.
