## 3. Record scope (applies to every new page and action, in the service layer)
- **Manager:** every customer.
- **Team Leader:** customers with a case in the team, an active assignment to a team agent, or **unrouted** ones (no active assignment and no case with an agent), so any leader can assign them.
- **Agent:** customers assigned to the agent or linked to the agent's cases. Everything attached to a customer (contacts, addresses, preferences, interactions, assignment) uses the same scope: outside it a page is 404 and a forged POST is 404 and changes nothing.
- **Customer:** only their own preferences (`/portal/preferences`).
- Forbidden actions (an agent assigning, a Team Leader picking another team's agent, a customer calling a staff action) return 403 and are written to the audit log as denied.

## 7. Where the build may differ from your diagrams (please check)
1. **Assignment is customer-level and separate from case assignment.** `agent_assignments` holds one active agent per customer; reassigning ends the old row. A case keeps its own assigned agent.
2. **Agent creates a customer = assigned to that agent** (otherwise the agent could not open the customer they just created).
3. **Team Leaders also see unassigned customers** (like the unrouted case queue), so they can assign them. Agents never see unassigned customers unless a case is theirs.
4. **Duplicate check also runs when a primary email or mobile is changed** (not only on creation). A reason is required to use a contact that belongs to someone else.
5. **"Name plus mobile" means:** same first and last name and a mobile ending in the same 7 digits (it catches a wrong prefix). Same mobile or same email alone is already a match.
6. **Walk-ins record only the Data Privacy consent.** "Terms" is recorded for portal accounts, as before.
7. **Interactions follow the customer scope** (an agent sees interactions of her customers, not only her own); "Logged by me" is a filter. The sample said "own" only.
8. **Interaction types for staff:** call, visit, SMS, email, chat, internal note. `portal` exists in the table for later automation. The sample's "outcome" field and "create a follow-up" are not built (follow-ups are P4). A note has direction "internal".
9. **A portal customer's login email is changed by the customer**, not by staff.
10. **Marketing consent is an append-only history** in `customer_consents`; the current value is the latest row per type.
11. **No screen edits or deletes an interaction** once saved (the table itself is not locked like the audit log).
12. **Segments** still show "not computed yet (P9)".
