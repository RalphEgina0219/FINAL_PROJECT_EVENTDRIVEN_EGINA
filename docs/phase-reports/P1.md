## 6. Where the build may differ from your diagrams (please check)
1. **Login table:** `crm_users` holds every login: the setup admin, staff and customers. Customers link to their `customers` record through `customer_accounts`, and staff link to `employee_references` through `employee_id`.
2. **Roles** are their own tables (`roles`, `permissions`, `role_permissions`); `crm_users.role` points to `roles.code`.
3. **Escalation table name:** escalations are stored in `ticket_escalations` (master-prompt name) and belong to `customer_cases`.
4. **Ratings and feedback are split:** `customer_ratings` holds one 1–5 rating per case (or, later, per order), and `customer_feedback` holds the written comment.
5. **New cases are unrouted:** they have no team and appear in **every** Team Leader's Customer Queue. Assigning an agent sets the case's team to the agent's team.
6. **Status rules:**
   - **Open → Assigned** happens only through assignment.
   - **Escalated** is entered only by "Escalate" (with a reason) and left only by a supervisor action: take over (→ In Progress), return to agent (→ Assigned, level 0), or resolve.
   - **Escalation rights:**
     - an agent can escalate only at level 0;
     - a Team Leader can escalate only level-1 cases, to the Manager;
     - the Manager handles level 2 and may also act on level 1.
   - **Customer reply** on a Waiting for Customer case moves it back to In Progress automatically.
   - **Reopen:** Resolved/Closed → In Progress within 7 days. A customer can reopen a Resolved case; only a TL or the Manager can reopen a Closed one.
   - **Auto-close:** Resolved → Closed after 3 days with no customer reply (scheduled job).
7. **While a case is escalated**, the assigned agent can only add internal notes.
8. **Routes (one per role):** `/crm/manager`, `/crm/team-leader`, `/crm/agent`, `/portal` (customer "My Profile"), and `/setup` for the System Administrator.
9. **Submit Inquiry** is interim (see §4).
10. **Attachments:** `attachments` is one generic table (`entity_type`, `entity_id`, plus `case_id` for access checks), not a ticket-only table.
