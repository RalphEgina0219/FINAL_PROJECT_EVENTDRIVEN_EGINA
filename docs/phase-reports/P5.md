## 1. What was done
| Area | What now works |
|---|---|
| **Inventory adapter (mock)** | `services/integration/inventoryAdapter.js`. Inventory is the only source of products, prices and stock. In mock mode the answers come from `fixtures/mockInventory.js` (16 grocery products, one low on stock, one out of stock, one inactive) and **every page that shows them carries a "MOCK Inventory" notice**. CRM has no endpoint, form or table write that changes a price or a quantity. `disabled` refuses with a clear message; `live` is P6. Every call is written to `integration_logs` with mode `mock`. |
| **Checkout re-check** | The order is created in **one transaction** that first asks Inventory again for current price and availability. It is **rejected** if a price changed ("the price of X changed from ₱a to ₱b"), a product is not sold any more, or stock is not enough ("only 3 left, you asked for 5"). Nothing is created or reserved, and the refusal is audited (`order_rejected`). After a price change the cart shows the new prices so the customer can confirm again. |
| **Reservation by API call** | After the check, stock is reserved through the adapter with the key `reserve:<order_id>`. The order only exists once Inventory accepted the reservation, so it starts as **Confirmed** with the reservation reference. Reservations are serialized, so two checkouts cannot take the last unit (tested with 3 simultaneous requests). |
| **Idempotency** | The `order_id` is generated when the form is shown. A re-submitted form (double click, refresh) returns the existing order: **no second order, reservation or event**. `SaleCompleted` is idempotent: one sale per order (unique `sales_order_id`), one event per target (unique idempotency key `SaleCompleted:<sale_id>`); completing twice changes nothing and says so. The database itself refuses both duplicates. |
| **Events** | Written to `integration_outbox` in the same transaction as the change (`OrderConfirmed`, `SaleCompleted`, `SaleCancelled`, `RefundRecorded`), one row per target, with the full envelope (event_id, event_type, schema_version, source, occurred_at, entity_id, correlation_id, idempotency_key, data). In mock mode each row is `mock_delivered`, `is_mock = 1`: **nothing is sent anywhere**. The dispatcher with retries and the dead-letter queue is P6. |
| **Orders** | Staff: list with scope and filters, **New order** (choose a customer, quantities, delivery address, promo code, optional payment), order page with items, payments, progress, and actions. Customer: **Shop**, **Cart**, **My Orders**, order page, **Order Tracking**. Statuses: Confirmed → Ready → Completed, or Cancelled. |
| **Payments** | No gateway. **Cash** (at the counter), **Cash on Delivery**, **GCash** and **Maya**, each with a typed reference number. GCash/Maya references are unique across all orders. COD is recorded at checkout as "to be collected" and filled in with the receipt number when the cash is collected. Everything except counter cash must be **verified by a staff member who did not record it** (the customer can enter their own GCash/Maya reference; any staff verifies it). The order is **Paid** only when verified payments cover the total, and a sale cannot be completed before. Recorded payments can be voided; verified ones cannot. |
| **Complete / cancel** | Completing creates the sale (`SALE-YYYYMMDD-0001`), marks the reservation Issued and publishes `SaleCompleted`. Cancelling (needs a reason) releases the stock and publishes `SaleCancelled`; it is refused once a payment is verified. A customer can cancel their own unpaid, confirmed order. |
| **Refunds (TC-14)** | Requested from a completed sale (amount up to what is still refundable, reason, optional returned items with condition, optional case). A Team Leader or the Manager **who did not request it** approves or rejects (reject needs a reason); then it is recorded (one `RefundRecorded` event) and the sale and order become partly or fully refunded. The requester can never approve: refused by the app (403, audited) and by the database `CHECK`. |
| **Promotions** | The Manager creates one (percent or fixed amount; whole order, one product or one category; minimum order; usage limit; dates). It waits for **approval by a Team Leader or Manager who is not the creator** (app and database `CHECK`). A code is applied at checkout (any letter case) and the discount is stored per line with one redemption record. |
| **Campaigns** | The Manager creates, activates, ends or cancels a campaign (`CMP-0001`) and groups promotions in it. Its results show **only what real orders can answer**: promotion uses, discount given, orders and completed sales. There are no "sent / opened" figures, because the CRM sends no messages yet. |
| **Dashboards, customer page** | Agent, Team Leader and Manager dashboards show real order figures (in progress, awaiting payment, payments to verify, sales today, refunds and promotions waiting, 14-day trend, best sellers, promotion uses). The customer's home lists their orders and running promotions. The customer page has a live purchase history with a "New order" button. |

Seed (still exactly **7 login accounts**): mock product snapshot, 4 promotions (active, active, waiting for approval, expired), 3 campaigns, and 8 demo orders (portal and staff, pickup and delivery, every payment method) in every state: completed with sales, confirmed, ready, a payment waiting to be verified, a COD to collect, a cancelled order, 3 refunds (requested, recorded, rejected) and 20 mock events. Every row is `is_demo = 1`. `npm run seed:clear` removes only those rows (and keeps any real order, sale, refund or event, plus a demo promotion and its campaign if a real order used it).

## 3. Record scope (service layer, every read and write)
- **Customer:** own orders, cart and payments only. **Agent:** orders of customers in her customer scope or created for her. **Team Leader:** orders of customers in the team's scope (including unrouted customers) or of the team. **Manager:** all. Another team's agent or Team Leader gets 404 on the order page and on forged cancel, payment and create requests; a customer gets 403 on staff pages; staff get 403 on the customer's cart.
- Promotions and campaigns: Manager creates; Team Leader and Manager approve promotions (never the creator); campaigns are Manager-only; agents cannot open promotions.
- Forbidden actions are 403 and audited (`access_denied`).

## 7. Where the build may differ from your diagrams (please check)
1. **An order exists only after the reservation succeeded.** There is no saved "draft" or "waiting for stock" order; a refused checkout leaves nothing behind. The status flow starts at Confirmed.
2. **No "Preparing" / "Out for delivery".** Order Tracking shows the blueprint steps only: placed, confirmed and stock reserved, payment verified, ready, completed (or cancelled).
3. **Payments are verified by a second person.** GCash, Maya and COD references (typed by hand) are checked by a staff member other than the recorder; counter cash counts at once.
4. **COD is a "to be collected" row** created at checkout and filled in with the receipt number when the cash is collected (the table has no field for the chosen payment method otherwise).
5. **One promotion per order**, applied to the whole order, one product or one category; no per-customer limit and no stacking.
6. **SC / PWD discounts are not built**: `sales_orders` has no place for the ID number the blueprint says must be captured. They need a schema change (shown first, as usual), so I left them out. Tell me if you want them.
7. **No delivery fee** and no VAT in the order total: `tax_total` stays empty and VAT is computed by Accounting, as decided.
8. **Refunds have three steps** (approve or reject, then "record" when the money was returned by hand). Only a Team Leader or the Manager decides and records.
9. **Cancelling** is allowed only before a payment is verified; after that the way out is complete-then-refund.
10. **Stock held = the order rows.** In mock mode "available" is mock stock minus what existing CRM orders hold, so a cancelled demo order frees stock by itself.
11. **Campaign results** are limited to promotion uses, discount, orders and sales; campaign "sending" is not built.
